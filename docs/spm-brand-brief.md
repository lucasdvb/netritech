# SPM: Brand Brief

## 1. Who SPM is

- **Name:** SPM (legal name Service Plus Monde Ltée). The brand is only "SPM".
- **Base:** SPM Tower, Rue Desroches, Port Louis, Ile Maurice. Website and email domain: spmservices.fr.
- **Since:** 2016.
- **What it does:** It builds dedicated, stable human teams, accelerated by AI, for European companies that outsource part of their activity. The offer in one line: "Un seul endroit pour recruter, payer, protéger et faire grandir vos équipes. L'intelligence artificielle en soutien. L'humain au centre."
- **Leadership shown in the book:** Virgile Henaut, Directeur Général (the stationery mockups carry his name).

## 2. Strategy

**Mission:** "Faire du travail une force humaine, augmentée." Give every client a team that truly knows their business, made faster and more precise by AI. "Pas un contrat de plus. Une équipe qui reste."

**Mission values:**
- Sérénité: teams that recruit fast, stay compliant and keep the human at the center.
- Lieu de vie: work that connects, values people and makes them want to come back.
- Prospérité personnelle: grow beyond the role, build beyond the contract.

**Positioning:** "SPM place l'humain au cœur du travail." A dedicated, stable team that masters your activity, with "la qualité d'une équipe interne spécialisée, au coût et à la rapidité de l'externalisation".

**Philosophy:** "Chaleur et sophistication." Warmth is the human, sophistication is the intelligence. SPM is intelligent without being cold, accessible without being ordinary.

**Promise:** "Avec SPM, vous ne gérez pas un contrat. Vous travaillez avec une équipe."
- Functional: measurable results (faster answers, better accuracy, fewer errors).
- Business: internal-team quality at outsourcing cost and speed.
- Emotional: the peace of mind of knowing people who truly know your business follow your activity.

**Differentiator:** "Là où le secteur vend de la capacité, SPM vend de la continuité, et fait de cette continuité la source directe de la performance."

**Commercial signature:** "La fusion de l'humain et de l'IA, au service de votre croissance."

**Four pillars:**
1. Continuité: teams stay in post, "La même équipe, chaque année."
2. Excellence humaine: expertise built over time, never through rotation.
3. Intelligence appliquée: AI for precise uses, never as a showpiece. "L'IA fait gagner le temps que l'humain investit ailleurs."
4. Résultats prouvés: every promise backed by a verifiable figure.

**Pitch:** One word: Continuité. One sentence: "SPM construit des équipes humaines, accélérées par l'IA, qui restent dans la durée."

## 3. Audiences

- **The European client:** A company in France or Europe that outsources part of its activity and wants more than volume: continuity, quality and a team that progresses with them.
- **The Mauritian talent:** A bilingual French-English professional in Mauritius looking for a real career rather than a stopgap job, with clear progression.
- **Employer brand:** "Exigence & Bienveillance." Exigence means aiming for excellence every time. Bienveillance means caring for people as much as results.

## 4. Brand architecture

SPM (parent brand) → HUMAN & AI (platform and signature, present in every expression) → expressions: Conseil stratégique, Support client, Opérations, Marque employeur.

Slogan: "Human & AI", in English, always. It is never translated.

## 5. Voice

Three principles, applied everywhere (sales, recruitment, social, internal):

1. **Comme un collègue:** the clarity of a competent colleague explaining a situation, never institutional emphasis.
2. **On prouve, on n'affirme pas:** every promise rests on a verifiable fact (a result, a figure, a named person), never a vague formula.
3. **On nomme les personnes:** talk about the teams doing the work, not the internal organization. The client always knows who they work with.

**Before/after examples from the book:**
- Avant: "Nous sommes leader de l'innovation dans l'externalisation." Après: "SPM construit des équipes qui restent, parce que c'est ce qui produit de vrais résultats."
- Avant: "Rejoignez une entreprise dynamique et innovante." Après: "Ici, l'IA vous enlève les tâches répétitives pour vous laisser progresser plus vite."
- Avant: "Notre IA révolutionnaire transforme votre service client." Après: "Notre IA traite ce qui peut l'être. Nos spécialistes gèrent tout ce qui compte vraiment."
- Avant: "Nous sommes fiers de notre équipe exceptionnelle." Après: "Trois ans sur le même compte. Voici ce que ça change pour notre client."

**Personality (if SPM were a person):** Fiable, Réactive, Humaine, Augmentée (uses AI to go further, never to hide behind it), Proche (accessible even as it grows).

**Synthesis:**
- SPM est: une équipe humaine, accélérée par l'IA, qui reste dans la durée.
- SPM n'est pas: un centre d'appel de plus, ni une promesse d'automatisation totale.
- SPM promet: une continuité qui produit de la performance, pas seulement de la capacité.
- SPM prouve: chaque promesse par un fait vérifiable.
- Five-year goal: a European client can say "La même équipe me suit depuis le premier jour, et elle n'a jamais été aussi rapide."

## 6. Logo

- **Concept:** The symbol joins two interlocked shapes (navy and teal "S" forms inside a diamond). They stand for the meeting of human and AI and a shared direction toward the future. The wordmark is a high-contrast serif "SPM" with "HUMAN & AI" in widely spaced capitals underneath.
- **Usage:** The logo must always appear on all SPM materials, products and services.
- **Versions:**
  - Navy version on light backgrounds.
  - White version on dark or steel blue backgrounds.
  - On photos, always white, placed on a dark area of the image.
- **Clear space:** "x" equals the height of the inner graphic element of the symbol, applied on all four sides.
- **Minimum size:** 30 mm wide in print, 90 px on screen. At very small print sizes, use the symbol alone.
- **Never:** stretch or distort, flip, rotate, change the colors, change the position between symbol and type, or alter their relationship.

## 7. Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Bleu Ardoise | #1B2A38 | Main dark, backgrounds |
| Bleu Nuit | #0D141F | Deepest dark, in the logo |
| Sarcelle | #22808A | Teal accent, in the logo |
| Gris Perle | #DADDE0 | Light neutral |
| Bleu Acier | #43617A | Mid blue |

- **Secondary color:** Blanc brume (#F9FAFB), used for visual breathing room.
- **Logo rule:** Bleu Nuit and Sarcelle must never be used as weakened tints next to the logo. Gradients are encouraged elsewhere.
- **Extended palette:** Many tints exist (Brume nordique, Givre bleu, Turquoise ardoise, Lagon sombre and others) for digital flexibility. Use them sparingly.
- **Recommended combinations:** Nuit / Sarcelle / Gris Perle; Sarcelle / Gris Perle / Ardoise; Nuit / Ardoise / Gris Perle.
- **Combinations to avoid:** All four examples stack Bleu Acier with other palette colors, so treat Bleu Acier as a supporting color, not one to stack.
- **Overall feel:** "sûre, épurée et minimaliste", conveying trust, stability, expertise, clarity, modernity and AI.

## 8. Typography

- **Libre Baskerville:** Serif for headlines. It conveys authority and seriousness. Italics are reserved for key words, to create visual breathing room and underline what matters.
- **Bricolage Grotesque 036:** Sans-serif for subtitles and body text, Light to Bold.

**Format A:**
- Headline: Libre Baskerville Bold 44 pt, leading 52, tracking -20.
- Subtitle: Bricolage Medium 13 pt, leading 18.2.
- Body: Bricolage Regular or Light 10 pt, leading 15.

**Format B:**
- Headline: Libre Baskerville Bold 72 pt, leading 78, tracking -30, with one or two words in Italic.
- Subtitle: Bricolage Light 20 pt, leading 28.
- Body: same as Format A.

## 9. Visual universe

- **Layout:** Clean and airy, with white space and colors dosed so they never overpower the photography.
- **Headline pattern:** A Libre Baskerville headline carries the message, with one key word in italic. Bricolage Grotesque carries the secondary information.
- **Illustration:** Flat, friendly line illustrations in the brand palette (navy, teal, grey), showing diverse people, headsets, laptops and plants.
- **Graphic device:** Thin outlined rounded chevron shapes in white and teal on navy, plus bold diagonal teal and grey shapes on covers.
- **Photography:**
  - Do: high quality, lifestyle and ambiance, employees who look natural, smiling and active, people interacting with technology, SPM's own offices where possible, and respect for copyright. Captions like "Conseil stratégique à distance" and "...en personne" are used.
  - Abstract images linked to the services (networks, glass structures) are encouraged.
  - Don't: clichéd staged poses, images unrelated to the services (pylons, pills), low-resolution images, or recropping subjects.
- **Resolution:** 300 dpi minimum for print, 72 dpi for digital.
