## 0. Four things to decide before I apply

1. ⚠️ **The three testimonials say "À inventer" (3.04–3.06, and A.32).** I won't write invented quotes with made-up names and companies.
   - Block 3.01 says "Chaque logo et chaque témoignage est publié avec l'accord écrit du client". Invented quotes would make that sentence false.
   - In France, invented customer testimonials can count as a misleading commercial practice, and that risk lands on SPM.
   - Options:
     - (a) **Hide the testimonial blocks** until SPM has real quotes (recommended; the page still works without them).
     - (b) Use real quotes from real clients, anonymised if needed ("Un acteur de l'assistance routière · France") with their written agreement.
     - (c) Replace them with a factual block (3 years average seniority, 500 collaborateurs, 7 languages).
   - A.32 also says "24h/24", which contradicts the real hours of 8h–20h.
2. ⚠️ **The word "marque" must go** (the client circled it on the cover, A.02 and A.04: "Ne pas utiliser marque – à remplacer", with no replacement given).
   - It appears about 20 times, including the hero line "formée à votre marque" and the SEO tags.
   - Proposal:
     - "formée à votre marque" → **"formée à votre activité"**
     - "au nom de votre marque" → **"au nom de votre entreprise"**
     - "protège votre marque" → **"protège votre image"**
     - Titles "Au service des marques…" and "La confiance des marques" → **"Au service des entreprises que vous connaissez déjà"** and **"Ils nous font confiance"**.
   - "Charte de marque" (the client's internal document) stays.
3. ⚠️ **Words for the people** (cover remark 1, unanswered).
   - The client's own black-ink corrections already use **conseillers** (A.49, 2.22, 2.24, the Human & AI text) for the people who answer customers, and **collaborateurs** (2.05, 2.18) for staff in general.
   - I'll apply that: no more "agents", including the simulator labels "Nombre d'agents" and "Coût chargé / agent / mois".
4. ⚠️ **Opening line** "La fusion de l'humain et de l'IA pour votre croissance." (written above block A.01 in both rounds).
   - Proposal: use it as the small line above the hero headline, and move "Service client, back-office, prospection et recrutement externalisés." into the SEO description.
