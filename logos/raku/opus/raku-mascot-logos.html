Bread that walks to your door.

Six hand-drawn mascot marks for RAKU, in the brand navy and cream. They share one family: a navy body, big cream eyes with a glint, a small smile, and a die-cut sticker border with a thin navy keyline, so every mark holds up on cream, on navy and on photos.

The six marks

Switch the background to see each mark the way it will sit on a cream box or a navy van.

The courier

The Walker

A plump bâtard with big eyes, on its way to your door. Closest to your reference and the easiest to own.

Best for Primary mark · app icon · box seal

The promise

The Dash

The same loaf in a hurry, leaning in with motion streaks cut into its back. It says the bread arrives while it is still warm.

Best for Delivery alerts · van livery · motion

The welcome

Bonzour

A sandwich slice waving hello (bonzour is Mauritian Creole for hello). The friendliest of the six, and the easiest to animate.

Best for Social · welcome cards · kids' range

The van

The Roller

A round boule on two wheels. Bakery and delivery van in one silhouette, with a sourdough score across the top.

Best for Van doors · order tracking · stickers

The stride

The Baguette

A long baguette striding along. Tall and diagonal, it fits narrow spaces where a round mark cannot.

Best for Paper bags · receipts · tall formats

The lockup

The Signature

RAKU in bouncy, chunky capitals merged into one die-cut sticker, with The Walker stepping out beside it. The full name for places that need it.

Best for Shopfront · website header · invoices

Small-size proof

At 48 and 24 pixels, the size of an app icon and a map pin. The Walker, Bonzour and The Roller stay readable smallest; The Baguette loses its legs first.
