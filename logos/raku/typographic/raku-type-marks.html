One letter, one loaf.

Twelve RAKU wordmarks and monograms built from real typefaces, each hiding a piece of bread in its letterforms: a counter, a stem, a crossbar, an accent. Every mark is one colour, so it works in navy on cream or cream on navy.

The twelve marks

Switch the background to see each mark on a navy van or a cream box.

01 Playfair Display

Le Pain

The R's counter becomes a scored baguette. The closest echo of your reference, in a confident Didone.

02 Unbounded

Crossbar

A baguette lies across the A as its crossbar. Wide and heavy, so it reads from across the street on a van.

03 Pacifico

Script Stem

The R's stem is scored like a baguette, the same move as Pañe. Warm and handwritten.

04 Yellowtail

Rakū

A little loaf sits over the u like an accent. Elegant script with a wink.

05 Shrikhand

Scored K

The k's tall ascender is scored like a baguette. Chunky, cheerful, old-bakery signage.

06 Fraunces

Little Roll

The ball at the end of the r becomes a bread roll with two cuts. A soft serif that feels hand-baked.

07 Gloock

Rising

The U is a bread tin and a scored loaf rises out of it, above the line. Freshly baked, every morning.

08 Bodoni Moda

The Slice

An R cut out of a slice of bread. Built for app icons, stamps and box seals.

09 Outfit

Baguette K

The k's stem is a standing baguette. Light, airy and modern, the most minimal of the twelve.

10 Instrument Serif

Signature

A long scored baguette underlines the name like a signature. Patisserie-level polish.

11 Syne

Long Counter

The wide R's long counter turns into a baguette. Contemporary and architectural.

12 DM Serif Display

The Seal

RA over KU in a double ring, with a baguette between the lines. A stamp for bags, boxes and wax seals.

All twelve typefaces are from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License or Apache 2.0, so they are free for commercial use. Each mark is supplied as an outlined SVG, so the font doesn't need to be installed to use the logo.
