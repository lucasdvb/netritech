## Intent

SPM builds dream teams for customer support (main), admin and sales. An 8-second silent scroll-scrub hero film in the "Agent Shadows" concept, as close as possible to the user's lorry reference film: golden-hero establish, crane rise, scan wave, the world draining to black, a dive into wireframe, ribbons converging, a hairline grid. AI prominent (data ribbons, agents), wow factor, minimal, sleek, premium, Apple-style. Humans first; AI is their shadow, never a replacement.

## Assets

- assets/first-frame.jpg — edited team photo (cool on-brand daylight), 1920x1080; the film opens exactly on it.
- assets/first-frame-1k.png — the same edit at the model's native 1672x941.
- assets/last-frame.png — dark SPM hairline grid; the film settles exactly on it and holds.

## Customizations

- Built entirely in code from the still photo (no AI video): 2.5D camera, local person cut-outs, a real scan line, teal agents made from each person's own silhouette as point-cloud and wireframe (no android look), silk data ribbons, drain to black, dive, ribbons converging, exact grid ending.
- Option B chosen: an empty-room background plate (one GPT Image 2.5 Flare edit, about 6 credits) so the camera can glide and rise with real parallax. Requires explicit approval of the exact prompt before running.
- HyperFrames is the render engine; Remotion skills' motion-design guidance informs the finish.
