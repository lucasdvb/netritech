# Moana Beauté: ideas backlog (sales and retention)

Parked by the owner on 2026-10-10 to focus on UI and UX first. Starred items were the recommended first five.

## Trust
1. ★ Real reviews with photos (e.g. Judge.me): stars on cards and product pages, review request after delivery.
2. ★ Authenticity proof on every product: sourcing, batch or expiry date, "100% authentic", plus a "How we source" page.
3. "Texture & finish" clips: a 5-second video of the product on a hand.
4. Free-from filters and badges (fragrance-free, alcohol-free, pregnancy-friendly, reef-safe SPF), from real ingredient lists only.

## Help her choose
5. ★ "Email me my routine" from the routine finder (captures subscribers at peak intent).
6. Shop by ingredient: ingredient pages (niacinamide, centella, rice, hyaluronic acid) and search by ingredient.
7. "Good for" chips on product cards (skin type, concern).
8. Compare two products side by side (texture, key ingredients, skin types, size, price per ml).

## Bigger baskets
9. ★ Routine sets at a small saving ("Glass skin starter", "Island sun kit").
10. ★ Free gift tiers in the bag ("Add Rs 400 for a free sheet mask"), next to the free-delivery bar.
11. Minis and travel sizes as add-ons.
12. "Pick a free sample" in the bag.

## Bring her back
13. Moana rewards: points for orders, reviews and birthdays.
14. Refill reminders by email, optional subscribe-and-save.
15. Back-in-stock alerts instead of a dead "Sold out".
16. Welcome offer for joining the Moana list (quiet bar or slide-in, never a blocking popup).

## Gifting
17. Gift wrap, gift notes and digital gift cards (gift card template already built).
18. Seasonal gift guides (Christmas, Divali, Eid, Mother's Day, birthdays) and budget guides.

## Content and community
19. The Journal: guides for island skin (humidity, SPF and the sea, routines for heat, K-beauty 101).
20. #MoanaGlow gallery: customer photos from Instagram, linked to products.

## Behind the scenes
- Shopify abandoned-cart and abandoned-browse emails.
- Google Shopping and Meta catalogue feeds.
- Local payment provider logos near Add to bag once connected.
