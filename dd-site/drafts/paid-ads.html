Before you choose

How to choose a Facebook or Google ads agency in Mauritius.

  1. 01

    Ask how they track results

    If an agency reports clicks and reach but can't tell you what each enquiry cost, you are paying for activity, not results.

  2. 02

    Check where your money goes

    Your ad budget should be paid to the platforms from your own account, separate from the agency's fee. Ask for that in writing.

  3. 03

    Ask what happens after the click

    Ads send people somewhere. Ask what page they land on and who answers them, because that is where most ad money is lost.

  4. 04

    Ask how often they look

    Campaigns drift. Ask how often someone checks and adjusts them, and what you will see in the report.

Questions

Ad questions, answered.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market and your offer. We start with a test budget we agree with you, then raise it only when the numbers justify it.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. You pay your ad budget directly to Meta, Google or TikTok. Our fee covers the planning, the ads, the tracking and the management, and we take no cut of your budget.

Facebook ads or Google ads: which is better?

They do different jobs. Google ads reach people who are already searching for what you sell. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who aren't searching yet but look like your customers. Many businesses need both, and we tell you where to start.

Do you guarantee results?

We set targets with you, such as a cost per enquiry, and report against them every month. Nobody can honestly guarantee what the platforms will do, so we don't.

How soon will I see results?

The first figures come in as soon as the ads go live. Every campaign needs a testing period before it settles, and we tell you what we are learning each month.

Do I keep my ad accounts?

[Yes. Your ad accounts and pages stay in your name and you give our team access. If we stop working together, everything stays with you. To confirm.]

Can you run ads in French and Creole?

Yes. We write ads in English, French and Creole, depending on who you want to reach.

Do you run TikTok ads?

Yes, when your customers are there. We look at where your customers spend their time before deciding where your budget goes.
