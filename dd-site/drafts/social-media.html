Before you choose

How to choose a social media agency in Mauritius.

  1. 01

    Ask to see the plan

    A good agency shows you what it will post and why before the month starts. If the posts just appear, nobody is steering.

  2. 02

    Ask who makes the content

    Find out who writes, designs and films, and whether the content is made for your business or adapted from a template.

  3. 03

    Ask who answers your messages

    Comments and messages are where customers ask to buy. Agree who replies, how fast, and how serious enquiries reach you.

  4. 04

    Be wary of follower promises

    Followers can be bought, and reach depends on the platforms. Ask what the agency commits to doing, not what it promises will happen.

Questions

Social media questions, answered.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, plus your Google listing. We recommend the ones your customers actually use.

How many posts will you publish?

It depends on your plan. Our standard plan includes four posts a week on each platform. We agree the number before we start.

Do I approve the posts?

Yes. You see the plan and the posts before they go live, and nothing goes out without your approval.

Do you film and take photos?

Some plans include shoots at your business or your events. In others, you send us your photos and videos and we turn them into finished posts. We agree which at the start, so there are no surprises.

Will you promise me more followers?

No. Reach on social media depends on platforms we don't control, so we don't promise follower numbers. We commit to the work: the plan, the posts, the replies and an honest report every month.

Do you post in French and Creole?

Yes. We write in English, French and Creole, whichever your customers speak.

How long before social media brings customers?

Social media builds trust over months, not days. Posting every week is what makes people recognise you. If you need enquiries quickly, we pair it with paid ads.

Can I still post myself?

Of course. We plan around anything you want to post yourself, such as news from your shop or your events, so your page stays consistent.
